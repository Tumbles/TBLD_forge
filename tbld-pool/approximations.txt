# Scripts that do almost, but not exactly, what Brett's text says: "Name | difference". For Brett's review.
Epic Confrontation | Only the creature you don't control is exiled if it would die; your own creature dies normally.
Frontier Siege | Khans adds GG at your first main phase (the text says once per turn, at your main phase).
Wish | You may play the exiled card this turn only.
Gift of Immortality | Returns only itself at the next end step, not every Aura that was attached.
Nevermore | Superiority (opponents with lower life reveal their hands) is not done; the naming is.
Warhorn Blast | The copy made when cast from exile uses the spell's copy rules; untested.
Annul | Counters an activated ability only if its source is an artifact or enchantment (the text says any activated ability).
Monastery Siege | Dragons gives ward 3 to your permanents but not to you.
Mystic Reflection | The copying effect lasts only this turn (the text has no limit).
Sidisis Faithful | The returned creature's name is not locked until your next turn.
Vodalian Mindsinger | You keep control as long as you control Vodalian Mindsinger (the text says as long as you control a Merfolk Wizard).
Aether Snap | Removes counters from permanents but not from players.
Defeat | Every opponent discards if you attacked this turn (the text: each opponent that was attacked).
Sludge Crawler | Its pump costs 2 (the text says 2/C, two or colorless).
Beamsplitter Mage | Copies the spell for one other creature (the text says up to two).
Bountiful Promenade | You add the mana yourself (the text says a player of your choice).
